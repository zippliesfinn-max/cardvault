Prices & grading

Raw EUR and USD estimates are shown when available from the Pokémon TCG API. PSA 10, BGS 10 and CGC 10 values need a licensed grading-price data provider and are not invented or estimated here.

Privacy

Your saved collection stays in this browser on this device. Searches and card lookups contact the Pokémon TCG API. There is no Gmail sign-in or cloud sync in this version.
